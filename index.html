About

I am an MSc student in Bioengineering at the University of Padua, with an academic background in computer engineering and an interest in the application of artificial intelligence to biomedical research and healthcare.

My current interests include machine learning for biomedical data, generative AI for medicine, medical imaging, computational genomics, multi-task learning, and trustworthy AI systems for clinical and scientific applications.
